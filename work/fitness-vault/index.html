The idea

MORE TIME
ON THE FLOOR.

Fitness Vault is my personal web platform project for independent gyms. The idea is simple: give daily gym operations a focused home, from the member register to the end-of-day overview.

The public website introduces the platform through a bold gym identity, clear feature explanations, and interface previews. Dark surfaces and bright green accents keep the visual language connected to the training floor.

A practical product story

  • Membership records and renewal workflows.
  • Member check-in and front-desk operations.
  • A daily closing overview.
  • Clear product previews that explain the interface.

These are the workflows presented by the project. The preview below uses fictional sample member records.

Decisions behind the design

  1. Start with the daily routine.

    Member records, check-in and closing tasks give the product story a practical order. Visitors meet the work the platform is intended to support before exploring the interface.

  2. Make the front desk concrete.

    The attendance preview brings member search, status and check-in controls into one view. It turns a feature list into a recognizable working screen.

  3. Keep the example useful and private.

    Fictional member records show how the interface reads without publishing a gym’s real customer data.

Where it stands

A live personal project bringing a gym-focused identity, a clear product overview, and front-desk interface examples together.
