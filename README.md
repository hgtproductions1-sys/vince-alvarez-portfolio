## What works

- Mobile navigation, project category filters, native FAQ disclosures, email-address copying.
- Email brief composer with required-field and whitespace validation. It opens the visitor’s configured email app; the visitor reviews and sends the draft. It does not send email or store inquiries.
- A procedural, pointer-responsive full hero 3D background with pause/play. All three concepts include their own themed 3D backgrounds and controls. Motion respects the system’s reduced-motion preference on startup. Rendering stops offscreen and in hidden tabs; pixel ratio is capped. A static design remains if WebGL is unavailable.
- FORME: project filters and accessible native project dialogs with Escape support.
- Slow Tide: synchronized room selection and a sample stay preview with date validation. No booking or payment occurs.
- Common Ground: sample-menu filters and a coffee recommendation picker.

## Optional sound

Use the bottom-right Sound off button to enable the quiet original ambient loop and paper/control effects. The page starts silent on every visit. Music pauses while the introduction video plays and while the tab is hidden; explicit mute is preserved through those transitions. Audio is independent of the motion preference, requires no external service, and loads only after opt-in. Concept pages and case studies remain silent.

Run `node scripts/check-audio.mjs` for audio lifecycle checks. `scripts/prepare-audio.mjs` reproduces the three locally authored MP3 files using FFmpeg (set `PORTFOLIO_FFMPEG` to an absolute encoder path if needed). Uncompressed masters are kept privately in `.qa/audio-masters/`, outside the publication.

Mouse entry into enabled controls adds a gentle synthesized pop after sound opt-in, with nested-target deduplication and a 120ms cooldown. It responds even while the music downloads; the switch shows Loading sound… until assets are ready. The original near-inaudible noise tick was replaced with a 75ms pop at about -27 dB peak after gain. Touch movement stays silent. A 40px cobalt SVG origami cat follows the mouse after the opening, using one resting animation loop and no new renderer. It fades over controls/fields, hides for keyboard navigation, touch input, hidden tabs, reduced motion and paused motion. Hide cat / Show cat remembers a separate local preference. Run `node scripts/check-cat.mjs` for lifecycle and pointer behavior checks.

The current music is `portfolio-chill.mp3`: an original 32-second, 60 BPM electric-piano and soft-bass loop with relaxed ninth chords and no sustained synth drone. Playback gain 0.2 gives approximately -39.5 dB mean, -22 dB peak. The old ambient master is retained locally but excluded from publication and the ZIP. Generate just the replacement with `node scripts/prepare-audio.mjs --music-only`.
