BEFORE
WE BUILD.

How fast can you deliver my website?＋

It depends on the number of pages, features, and whether your content is ready. Send me your brief and I’ll propose a realistic timeline before we start. Focused scope and prompt feedback help us move quickly.

Do you build custom sites or WordPress?＋

Both. I can create a custom website or work with WordPress, depending on how you want to manage content and what your website needs to do.

Can I edit my website myself?＋

Yes. I can add a private editor where you create articles, edit text and images, save drafts, and preview before publishing. We’ll agree on the content you need to manage and choose a CMS that fits your site, with a walkthrough at handover.

Can you help with an existing website?＋

Yes. I can help with content updates, layout improvements, WordPress maintenance, and website quality checks. Share the URL and the changes you have in mind.

What do you need from me?＋

A short description of your business, your goals, examples you like, and any existing content or brand assets. We’ll work through the remaining details together.

Can I use my own domain name?＋

Yes. A custom domain is optional and quoted separately. Registration and annual renewal prices vary by the name, extension, availability, and registrar. I’ll confirm both costs before any purchase.

What about hosting and support?＋

For eligible static websites, I can include Cloudflare Pages setup, a free pages.dev website address, and 12 months of basic deployment assistance. Your hosting account stays in your name. Free-tier hosting is subject to provider terms and limits and may remain free after the first year. Content updates, new features, CMS services, and any paid upgrades are quoted separately.

Are the concept projects real client work?＋

They’re original design studies for fictional businesses, made to show different approaches. Fitness Vault and The Faceless Prompts are my live personal projects.
